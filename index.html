Privacy Policy for Vocav

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vocav ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Apple or Google, we collect your name, email address, and authentication identifier to create and manage your account.
  • Learning & Usage Data: We store your vocabulary progress, spaced repetition history, and app preferences to synchronize your learning experience across sessions.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics Data: We use Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to collect anonymized device information, app interactions, session duration, and crash/performance diagnostics to improve app stability and user experience.
  • AI Chat & Voice Inputs: Text and voice messages you submit during AI language practice are processed to generate responses and audio feedback.
  • Subscription & Purchase History: In-app purchases and subscription statuses are processed by Apple App Store and managed via RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your credit card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and personalize vocabulary learning and AI conversation features.
  • To verify subscription entitlements (Vocav / LangApp Pro) and restore purchases via RevenueCat.
  • To analyze app usage trends, monitor performance, and fix bugs using Firebase Analytics.
  • To ensure account security and respond to support requests.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party service providers to operate and analyze our app, including:

  • Authentication: Apple (Sign in with Apple) and Google (Google Sign-In).
  • Analytics: Google Firebase Analytics (used strictly for app analytics and performance monitoring, not for cross-app advertising tracking).
  • Subscriptions: RevenueCat (for purchase validation and entitlement management).
  • AI & Cloud Infrastructure: Google Gemini, Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, and cloud hosting/storage providers.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the app settings or by contacting us via email.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@vocav.app
